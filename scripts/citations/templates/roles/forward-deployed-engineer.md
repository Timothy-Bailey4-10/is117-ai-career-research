## What it is

An FDE works directly with a customer to make an AI product work inside that customer's real environment. The defining line: a role built around **building and delivering after the sale** is real FDE work, while one built around demos and supporting the sales process is sales engineering {{s_after}}.

The model comes from Palantir {{f_pal}}. LinkedIn's January 2026 report found FDE roles grew **42×** between 2023 and 2025, compared with 13× for AI engineer roles {{t_42}}.

## What the work involves

- **Discovery and scoping**: OpenAI lists discovery and technical scoping as part of owning a deployment {{m_disc}}
- **Integration**: across postings, the common thread is integration work {{m_int}}
- **Production code**: explicitly required in 12 of 14 postings reviewed {{ik_req}}
- **Evals and adoption**: OpenAI measures success through production adoption and eval-driven feedback {{m_adopt}}
- **Travel**: expect 40–60% travel in many forward deployed AI roles {{pa_travel}}

## The interview loop

Paraform's recommended FDE loop {{ph_loop}}:

| Stage | What it tests |
|---|---|
| Recruiter screen | Motivation and customer-facing communication |
| Practical integration task | Cleaning messy data and wiring up an integration, not algorithm puzzles |
| System design under constraints | Working with legacy databases and incomplete docs |
| Decomposition / case study | Scoping an underspecified problem (the signature FDE stage) |
| Behavioral | Handling stakeholder conflict and ambiguity |

## How to break in

1. Build one AI project for a real user. A strong project includes an **evaluation dataset, defined success criteria, and failure categories** {{ik_proj}}.
2. Write it up with the problem, architecture, eval results, and what you'd do next. This becomes your story for the case-study round.
3. Apply to the related titles too: Applied AI, Deployment, Field, and Solutions Engineer {{t_titles}}.

::: warning Red flag
Companies use the FDE title for three different jobs {{b_three}}, and some relabel solutions engineering roles as FDE {{p_se}}. Ask: *"How much of this role is writing production code after the sale, and is any of the pay tied to a quota?"*
:::
