# Find the Jobs

## Search terms

These titles are often used for FDE-like work {{t_titles}}. Copy them into LinkedIn, Indeed, or a careers page:

```text
"forward deployed engineer" OR "forward deployed AI engineer"
"applied AI engineer" OR "deployment engineer"
"field engineer" AI OR "solutions engineer" AI
"forward deployed" new grad OR entry level OR intern
```

## Check every posting for

- [ ] **Post-sale building**: a real FDE builds and delivers after the sale. Demo-heavy roles are sales engineering {{s_after}}
- [ ] **No quota**: 0% of FDE postings in a 1,000-posting sample carried one {{b_quota}}
- [ ] **Travel**: 40–60% is common {{pa_travel}}
- [ ] **Equity**: 70% of postings mention it {{b_equity}}
- [ ] **Location**: SF medians run 8% above remote {{r_sf}}

## Your application kit

1. **One shipped AI project** with an evaluation dataset, success criteria, and failure categories {{ik_proj}}
2. **A one-page write-up** covering the problem, architecture, eval results, and what you'd change
3. **A 5-minute demo** you can give to a non-technical audience
4. A resume that leads with **impact on users**, not the list of tools you used
