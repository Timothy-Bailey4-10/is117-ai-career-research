## What they do

- Write **product policies** — what the AI should refuse or allow
- Design **moderation** systems
- Run **red-team** exercises to find harmful behavior before users do
- Respond to **misuse** incidents

## Related role: AI Compliance

AI Compliance advisors (often lawyers) turn new AI regulations into product go/no-go decisions and review contracts. It's a growing specialty for people with legal or privacy backgrounds.

## How to break in

People from content-moderation policy, platform trust & safety, and security incident response move in most naturally. Students can start with courses in **ethics, policy, or cybersecurity**, plus hands-on red-teaming practice.
