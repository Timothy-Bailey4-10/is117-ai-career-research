## What they do

An AI Engineer **builds products on top of AI models**. Instead of inventing new models (that's research), they take existing models and make them useful: connecting them to company data, tuning them, wrapping them in apps, and shipping them to users.

## Common tasks

- **RAG** (retrieval-augmented generation) — let a model answer from company documents
- **Fine-tuning** — adjust a model for a specific task
- **Agents** — let a model call tools and take actions
- **Prompting** — design the instructions a model follows
- **Deploying** — package and run it in the cloud

## Education and experience

- About **1 in 4** postings don't require a degree.
- Most roles want **2–6 years** of experience; only about **2.5%** target 0–2 years.
- California has about a third of postings, then Texas and Washington.

## Pay

The same study reported an average of about **$206K**, ranging from roughly **$143K** for the newest hires to **$269K+** for 10+ years.

## How to break in

1. Get strong in **Python**.
2. Build **one RAG app** over real documents (class notes, a club's handbook).
3. Add an **evaluation** step that scores its answers.
4. Deploy it publicly and write up what you learned.
5. Since few postings are entry-level, aim first for **software engineering internships** on teams that use AI.
