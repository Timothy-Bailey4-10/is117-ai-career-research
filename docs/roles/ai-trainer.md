## What they do

AI Trainers **create the data that teaches and tests AI models**:

- Writing example answers and ranking model responses (RLHF)
- Building **evaluation sets**
- **Red-teaming** — trying to make the model fail
- Writing **rubrics** for what a good answer looks like

## Why it's a good entry point

This role values **subject expertise** over coding. Experts in medicine, law, math, biology, or writing are in demand because models need expert-quality examples. Guides report subject-matter experts can move in within about **4–8 weeks**.

## How to break in

1. Pick your strongest subject.
2. Write 20 hard questions in that subject with model-quality answers and a grading rubric.
3. Use that as a portfolio sample when applying.
