## What they do

An AI PM decides **what an AI feature should and shouldn't do**. Compared with a normal PM, they spend extra time on:

- **Eval criteria** — what counts as a "good" answer?
- **Failure modes** — what happens when the AI is wrong, and how bad is it?
- **Trade-offs** — speed vs. accuracy vs. cost

## How to break in

- Existing PMs reportedly transition in about 4–8 weeks, mostly by building a **portfolio**.
- Students: write a one-page **spec** for an AI feature (problem, users, what "good" means, risks), then build a rough prototype.
