# The New AI Roles

The AI industry has split "AI work" into many specialized jobs. They fall into two broad groups:

- **Building AI** — engineers who write the code that makes AI systems work.
- **Shaping AI** — people who decide what AI should do, train it, and keep it safe.

## How the roles connect

## Compare the roles

| Role | Main job | Coding level | Talks to customers? | Reported US base pay* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [AI Engineer](./ai-engineer) | Build LLM-powered products | High | Sometimes | ~$160K–$200K most common range |
| [Forward Deployed Engineer](./forward-deployed-engineer) | Ship AI inside a customer's systems | High | **Every day** | Varies widely; top postings ~$280K |
| [Agent Engineer](./agent-engineer) | Build tool-using AI agents | High | Rarely | ~$185K–$320K |
| [AI Evaluation Engineer](./ai-evaluation-engineer) | Test and measure AI quality | Medium–High | Rarely | Emerging — few public bands |
| [AI Operations](./ai-operations) | Keep deployed AI running | Medium–High | Rarely | ~$155K–$275K |
| [AI Product Manager](./ai-product-manager) | Define what AI should do | Low–Medium | Often | ~$160K–$320K |
| [AI Trainer](./ai-trainer) | Create training and test data | Low | Rarely | ~$95K–$180K |
| [AI Trust & Safety](./ai-trust-safety) | Policy, red-teaming, misuse | Low–Medium | Sometimes | ~$175K–$300K |

<small>*Ranges come from 2026 industry guides listed on the [Sources](/sources) page. Most target experienced hires; entry-level pay is lower.</small>

## Which one fits you?

| If you like… | Look at… |
|---|---|
| Writing code and building products | AI Engineer, Agent Engineer |
| Solving problems with real people, travel, fast pace | Forward Deployed Engineer |
| Finding bugs and proving things work | AI Evaluation Engineer |
| Keeping systems running, on-call, DevOps | AI Operations |
| Planning, writing, deciding priorities | AI Product Manager |
| Deep knowledge in one subject (medicine, law, math) | AI Trainer |
| Ethics, policy, protecting people | AI Trust & Safety |
