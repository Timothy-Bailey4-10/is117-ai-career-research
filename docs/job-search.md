# Find the Jobs

## Search terms

Copy these into LinkedIn, Indeed, or a company careers page:

```text
"forward deployed engineer" OR "forward deployed AI engineer"
"applied AI engineer" OR "AI deployment engineer"
"solutions engineer" AI agents
"forward deployed" new grad OR entry level OR intern
```

## Where to look

| Where | Why |
|---|---|
| Company career pages (Anthropic, OpenAI, Palantir, Salesforce, Databricks, Scale AI, Sierra) | Most current listings, often posted first here |
| LinkedIn and Indeed | Widest coverage. Set alerts for the terms above |
| Startup boards (Wellfound, YC Work at a Startup) | Many early-stage AI companies hire FDEs as early engineers |
| Recruiter guides (Paraform, Recruiting from Scratch) | Pay benchmarks and hiring trends |

## Check every posting for

- [ ] **Production code**: is most of the job building, or demos and slides?
- [ ] **Success metric**: customer outcomes (good) or revenue and quota (sales role)?
- [ ] **Travel**: how many days per month on site?
- [ ] **Clearance**: required for many government-facing roles
- [ ] **Comp**: base, equity, and location. Remote usually pays ~7–8% less than SF

## Your application kit

1. **One shipped AI project** with real users and measured results
2. **A one-page write-up**: problem, architecture, evals, what you'd change
3. **A 5-minute demo** you can give to a non-technical audience
4. A resume that leads with **impact on users**, not the list of tools you used
